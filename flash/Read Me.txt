Welcome to the Commodore 64 desktop!

A tribute to the 1987 Macintosh Finder, on a Commodore 64 with a WiC64.

- Point with the mouse (or a joystick in port 2).
- Double-click icons to open them.
- Drag icons to folders, or to the Trash.
- Click an icon and type to rename it.
- Open a network share's icon to connect to it; drag it to the Trash to disconnect.
- The Command key is C= (Alt on a PC keyboard).
